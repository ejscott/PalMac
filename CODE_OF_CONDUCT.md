# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

PalMac contributors, maintainers, and community members are expected to make
participation respectful, welcoming, and harassment-free.

Positive behavior includes:

- showing empathy and patience;
- giving constructive, technically grounded feedback;
- respecting different experience levels and viewpoints;
- accepting responsibility and correcting mistakes;
- prioritizing community safety over winning an argument.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, threats, discriminatory language,
sexualized attention, deliberate intimidation, doxxing, spam, or publishing
another person's private information without permission.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or
permanently restrict participation when behavior is inappropriate, unsafe, or
harmful. Enforcement decisions should explain the reason when practical while
protecting the privacy and safety of affected people.

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository maintainer's GitHub
profile or, when the concern also involves a security issue, through private
vulnerability reporting. Do not use public issues to disclose private details.

## Scope

This policy applies in project spaces and when someone publicly represents the
project.
